# Mithaq Platform

Mithaq is a modern legal-tech platform designed to simplify digital contract creation, electronic signatures, approval workflows, and contract lifecycle management through a secure and centralized system that helps protect rights and organize contractual relationships professionally.


# Overview

Mithaq was developed to modernize traditional contract workflows by transforming them into a fully digital and secure experience that combines legal organization, electronic approvals, secure collaboration, digital signatures, and contract lifecycle management within one platform.

The platform enables individuals and organizations to create contracts, manage multiple parties, organize legal clauses, assign permissions, review agreements, and complete electronic signature operations through a professional and centralized workflow.

Mithaq helps users:
- Protect contractual rights
- Reduce paperwork and manual processes
- Organize legal documentation securely
- Simplify contract creation and approvals
- Improve collaboration between parties
- Reduce operational delays
- Track contract modifications and approvals
- Support digital transformation in legal workflows

# Core Features

## 1-Smart Contract Creation
Create contracts through a guided multi-step workflow with structured validation, organized legal sections, and dynamic contract generation.

## 2-Dynamic Party Management
Add and manage multiple contract parties with customized legal roles and permissions.

## 3-Secure Electronic Signature Workflow
Send secure digital signature invitations and manage approval workflows electronically through organized email-based operations.

## 4-Permission Management
Each contract party can have customized permissions such as:
- Viewing contracts
- Adding comments
- Editing clauses
- Uploading attachments
- Signing electronically

## 5-Contract Clauses System
Organize contracts into structured legal sections:
- Obligations & Agreements
- Penalties & Compensation
- Termination Conditions
- General Legal Clauses

## 6- Modification Workflow
Handle contract modification requests through organized review and approval operations between all involved parties.

## 7-Contract Status Tracking
Track contract lifecycle states including:
- Draft
- Pending Review
- Pending Signatures
- Approved
- Signed
- Cancelled

## 8-Dashboard & Analytics
Professional dashboard displaying:
- Contract statistics
- Active contracts
- Pending signatures
- Workflow progress
- User activities
- Subscription information

## 9- Notifications
Integrated email workflows for:
- Signature invitations
- Approval notifications
- Contract updates
- Verification emails
- Modification requests

## 10-Responsive User Experience
Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
